Common questions

How many events are free? Up to 3 events and the small widget. Nigh Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks unlimited events, medium and Lock Screen widgets, extra reminders, birthday import and all card colors.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the paywall and tap “Restore Purchase”.

How do I add a widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Nigh.

My events are missing on a new device. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled, and that iCloud sync is on in Nigh → More.
